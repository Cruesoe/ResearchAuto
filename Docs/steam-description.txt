[b]Whenever nothing is being researched, the next logical project starts automatically: the lowest tech level first, then the cheapest.[/b]

If you've put thousands of hours into RimWorld, you probably don't care exactly what's next; you just want the research to continue. Unlike similar mods, this one respects tech levels before cost, so your tribe won't stumble into Microelectronics before it has mastered the Bow.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]When the research slot is empty, the cheapest available project from the lowest remaining tech level starts, with ties picked at random.
[*]A project that already has progress is finished before a new one is started.
[*]A new project is picked shortly after the last one finishes, and never while the research tab is open.
[*]Anomaly, Gravship and Monolyn knowledge research run as their own queues alongside normal research.
[*]A letter tells you when every available project is done, so you know it's time to advance your tech level or build better research benches.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Enable Auto-Research[/b]: turns automatic picking on or off.
[*][b]Show Notifications[/b]: shows a message each time a project starts.
[*][b]Restrict to Current Era[/b]: only picks projects at or below your faction's tech level.
[*][b]Ignore Tech Level[/b]: picks by cost alone, across all tech levels.
[*][b]Prioritize Expensive Projects[/b]: picks the most expensive project instead of the cheapest.
[*][b]Include Anomaly, Gravship and Monolyn Techs[/b]: turns each parallel queue on or off.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Not compatible with Research Whatever or Research Queue, because they also pick the next research project.
Safe to add to an existing save.
